Features

Everything you need for polished recordings

DeskRec handles the fiddly parts of screen recording automatically, so you stay in flow.

Automatic zoom

Focus follows your actions. Movement or clicks smoothly zoom in on the right part of the screen, so viewers always see exactly what matters.

Cursor smoothing

Write and draw in marker strokes directly on the recording. DeskRec highlights your actions with clean, elegant annotations.

Built-in editor

Trim, replay and fine-tune right after you stop the recording. Export a clean MP4 without ever opening another tool.

Webcam bubble

Keep a floating webcam bubble on any part of the screen. A personal touch that helps your viewers connect with you.

Countdown overlay

An optional full-screen countdown gives you time to get ready before the camera starts rolling — nobody likes a rushed intro.

Distraction-free mode

Hide desktop icons automatically while recording for a clean, professional frame. Your viewers see your work, not your clutter.

How it works

Record, and you're already done

DeskRec was designed so the recording you end with is the recording you ship.

1 · Pick your source & go

Choose a full screen, a single window, or a custom region. Toggle system audio, microphone and webcam in one glance.

  • 15, 30 or 60 FPS capture for the motion you need.
  • Optional countdown before recording starts.
  • Speaker notes saved with every clip that you can keep on screen.

2 · DeskRec polishes as you record

Auto-zoom, cursor smoothing and highlights are applied in real time — the magic happens while you talk, not after.

  • Automatic zoom that follows clicks and movement.
  • Distraction-free mode hides desktop clutter.
  • A floating control pill lets you pause, resume or stop anytime.

3 · Review, then export MP4

Open the built-in editor, replay your clip, and export a crisp MP4 ready for Slack, YouTube or your docs.

  • Playback and trimming built right into the flow.
  • Clean MP4 export — shareable anywhere.
  • Cross-platform-friendly output for your team.
