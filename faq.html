DeskRec is a native Windows screen recorder created as a friendly Screen Studio alternative. It records your screen with automatic zoom, cursor smoothing and annotations, then lets you review and export a clean MP4 in one app.
DeskRec runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11 on any reasonably modern PC. Because it uses efficient native capture, it stays lightweight even while recording at 60 FPS.
Automatic zoom analyzes your clicks and mouse movement while you record and smoothly frames the active part of the screen. Viewers always follow your action without you moving the camera or editing afterwards.
