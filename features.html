DeskRec features, in detail

Every capability is designed around one idea: the recording you stop with is the recording you ship.

Automatic zoom

Whether it's a tiny dropdown, a code editor line, or a settings panel, DeskRec recognises the action and frames it beautifully.

  • Zooms in on clicks and significant mouse movement.
  • Smooth spring-based pan and zoom — never janky.
  • Traditional "follow the cursor" is the default, so nothing breaks.

Cursor smoothing & annotations

Your cursor gets a gentle, natural feel, and you can annotate directly over the recording as you narrate.

  • In-marker drawing to highlight any part of the screen.
  • Smooth cursor easing reduces dizziness in fast demos.
  • Clean visually-pleasing strokes that read well even compressed.

Capture sources

DeskRec adapts to whatever you're recording, from a full multi-monitor setup to a focused window.

  • Full screen or multi-display capture.
  • Single-window capture for distraction-free demos.
  • Custom region selection with a live preview.

Audio & webcam

Layer the sound and presence you need for a personal, human recording.

  • System audio capture for speaker sound and apps.
  • Microphone support with device selection.
  • Floating webcam bubble — position it anywhere on screen.

The built-in editor

No need for a separate video app. Review your clip, trim the extras and export straight away.

  • Playback with golden timeline controls.
  • Pause/resume during recording for controlled takes.
  • One-click MP4 export that's ready to share.

Power user niceties

Small touches that make a daily tool feel great to live in.

  • Distraction-free mode hides desktop icons automatically.
  • Countdown overlay so you're never caught off-guard.
  • Speaker notes saved alongside every clip.
