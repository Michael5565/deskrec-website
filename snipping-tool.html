More than a screenshot — a powerful snipping tool

You already know the basics: press PrtScn to grab the whole screen, or open the built-in snipping tool to snip a region, window, or freehand area. The problem is those are all static pictures. When you need to show a bug, demo a workflow, or teach a lesson, a screenshot isn't enough.

DeskRec is a native Windows snipping tool that goes further. Draw a region to capture a snip just like you always have — then go one step further and record that same region. One tool covers print screen, Snip & Sketch, Lightshot-style region capture, and full screen recording.

What you can do

Snip a region

Drag to snip any area of the screen — perfect for the quick captures Lightshot and Snip & Sketch users already love.

Record full screen or a window

Capture your whole screen, one window, or a custom region as a clean MP4 — not just a still image.

Add audio & webcam

Combine system sound, your microphone, and a webcam bubble so your snips explain themselves.

Edit & export

Review, trim, and export a polished MP4 that plays in Slack, Teams, YouTube, and your docs.
